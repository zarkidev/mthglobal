Funding Your Studies

Scholarships

MTH Global Education helps you apply for both fully funded and partial scholarships at Chinese universities.

Top Award

Fully Funded

  • Tuition covered by the scholarship
  • Monthly living stipend
  • On-campus accommodation
  • Competitive — strong academics improve your chances
Most Common

Partial Scholarship

  • Tuition reduction (first-year or full duration)
  • More seats and wider choice of universities
  • Lower academic entry requirements
  • Still dramatically cheaper than studying at home abroad
Honest guidance: scholarship results depend on the university and your academic record. We give you transparent information about fees, costs and realistic scholarship chances before you commit to anything.
